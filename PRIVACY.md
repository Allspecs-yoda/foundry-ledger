# Night Shift Foundry — Privacy Policy

**Effective:** 2026-08-27  
**Seller:** Dakota / Night Shift Foundry (@Allspecs-yoda)  
**Catalog:** [foundry-ledger](https://github.com/Allspecs-yoda/foundry-ledger)

This policy covers information collected when you buy a foundry SKU or watch this public catalog. It does not cover GitHub’s or Stripe’s own processing as independent controllers.

## 1. Who we are

Night Shift Foundry sells file-only digital packs. Checkout is Stripe. Delivery is public GitHub. There is **no email list**. Tail-buyer notify is Watch this repo, `feed.xml`, `catalog.json`, and CLAIM issues.

## 2. What we collect

**From Stripe Checkout** (required to pay):

- Payment method details (handled by Stripe; we do not store full card numbers)
- Billing name and address
- Phone number (requested at checkout)
- Email Stripe already has for the payment
- Charge amount, SKU metadata, receipt last-4, timestamps

**From GitHub** (only if you choose to interact):

- Public GitHub username
- Issue text you file (`CLAIM:`, `BRIEF:`, `REFUND:`)
- Stars / watches on public repos

Do **not** put phone or email in a public GitHub issue. If you start a custom build from the Workshop tab, we file **name, phone, and email** from Stripe (or a private message) in a **private** job log so we know what was built for whom. That log is not on GitHub.

We do **not** run analytics pixels on this ledger. We do not buy or sell marketing lists.

## 3. Why we use it

- Take payment and prevent fraud
- Attribute charges to a SKU in the public `CAPITAL.md` ledger (amounts, not your card)
- Match a CLAIM issue to a receipt last-4
- Fulfill a paid custom brief
- Comply with tax and bookkeeping law

Legal bases we rely on: contract (the purchase), legitimate interests (fraud, accounting), and legal obligation (tax records).

## 4. Sharing

- **Stripe** processes payments. See [Stripe’s privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).
- **GitHub** hosts the files and issues. See [GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).
- We do not sell personal information. We do not share buyer emails for advertising.

Public ledger rows show SKU, price, and decision — not your name, email, or address.

## 5. Retention

Stripe records follow Stripe’s retention. CLAIM issues stay on GitHub until you or we close/delete them under GitHub’s tools. Capital accounting rows are append-only and do not include your contact details.

## 6. Your choices

- Do not pay if you do not want to give Stripe billing details.
- Watch / unwatch the ledger to start or stop GitHub notifications.
- Do not file a CLAIM issue if you do not want your GitHub identity next to a last-4.
- Request deletion of a CLAIM issue body by opening `PRIVACY: delete claim` on this repo (accounting rows of dollar amounts stay).

## 7. Children

Packs are not directed at children under 13. Do not buy if you are under 13.

## 8. International

Checkout and GitHub may process data in the United States. If you buy from elsewhere, you consent to that transfer for the purchase.

## 9. Changes

Updates are committed to this file on `main`. The version in effect when you pay is the one that applies to that purchase.
